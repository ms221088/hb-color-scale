Hb Colour Scale Scanner

WHO Haemoglobin Colour Scale reader for ANMs • works offline

1. Photograph the colour scale with the blood strip

Put the blood strip behind the scale so the stain shows through the holes. Wait about 30 seconds, then photograph straight away. Hold the phone parallel to the card, 15–20 cm away. Avoid direct sunlight, shadows (including your own) and glare on the card.
Fit the whole colour scale inside the box • hold steady

2. Tap four spots, in order

STEP 1Tap a WHITE area (white paper or white strip) as the lighting reference.
STEP 2Tap the DARKEST shade (14 g/dL), beside the hole, not on it.
STEP 3Tap the LIGHTEST shade (4 g/dL), beside the hole.
STEP 4Tap the BLOOD STAIN seen through a hole.

Medical screening disclaimer: This tool is a screening aid only and is not a diagnostic device. The colour scale is an approximate method and phone-camera reading adds further error from lighting, camera and technique. Results must never replace a laboratory or HemoCue haemoglobin test or clinical judgement. Anyone with a low or critical reading, with symptoms (breathlessness, pallor, dizziness) or who is pregnant should be referred to a Medical Officer / health facility for confirmatory testing and treatment. Follow national (MoHFW / Anemia Mukt Bharat) protocols.
